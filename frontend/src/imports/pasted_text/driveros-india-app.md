Design a modern web + mobile application called "DriverOS India".  CORE VISION  DriverOS is a Digital Operating System built specifically for Indian truck drivers.  It replaces paper-based work, fragmented documents, and unorganized transport management with a single digital platform that helps truck drivers manage:  - Driving documents - Vehicle documents - Compliance & legal requirements - Trip-related records - Expenses - Health information - Emergency support - Government policy updates  This is NOT a general consumer app.  This is a REAL-WORLD productivity and compliance system for Indian truck drivers working in highways, logistics, and transport industries.  ==================================================== TARGET USERS ====================================================  Primary User: - Indian Truck Drivers (long-distance & local)  Secondary Users: - Fleet owners - Transport contractors - Logistics companies - Helpers / co-drivers  Context: - Highway travel - Inter-state transport - Remote areas - Low connectivity zones - Multilingual users - Low digital literacy users  ==================================================== DESIGN STYLE ====================================================  - Government + Logistics SaaS hybrid UI - Extremely simple and readable - High contrast for road usage - Mobile-first (Android heavy usage) - Dashboard-first UX - Minimal steps for actions - Offline-first support indicators - Blue + White + Orange accents - Large buttons, easy navigation - Voice-first friendly UI  ==================================================== MAIN DASHBOARD (CRITICAL SCREEN) ====================================================  This is the driver’s CONTROL CENTER.  Top Section: - Driver Name + Photo - Truck Number - Current Trip Status - Language Selector  Critical Alerts Section (MOST IMPORTANT): - License Expiry Date - Truck Insurance Expiry - PUC Expiry - Fitness Certificate Expiry - Permit Expiry - Upcoming Service Due  Highlight urgency: - Red = Immediate risk - Yellow = Upcoming - Green = Safe  Trip Section: - Current Route - Start → Destination - Distance remaining - Fuel status (if available)  Government Updates Section: - New highway rules - Toll updates - State transport notifications - Truck regulation changes - Insurance policy updates  Quick Actions: - Upload Document - Start Trip - End Trip - Emergency SOS - Find Fuel Station - Find Rest Stop (Dhabha) - Find Mechanic - Call Fleet Owner  ==================================================== DOCUMENT VAULT (TRUCK DRIVER FOCUS) ====================================================  Store and manage:  Driver Documents: - Driving License (Heavy Vehicle License) - Aadhaar - PAN  Vehicle Documents: - RC (Registration Certificate) - Insurance - PUC Certificate - Fitness Certificate - Permit (National / State)  Features: - Scan & Upload - Auto expiry detection - Renewal reminders - Share with owner / police / checkpoint - Offline document access indicator  ==================================================== COMPLIANCE CENTER ====================================================  Show legal compliance status for truck operations:  - License validity - Insurance validity - Permit validity - Fitness certificate status - PUC status  Compliance Score (0–100)  Risk Level Indicator: - Safe - Warning - High Risk (cannot operate legally)  Timeline: - Upcoming renewals - Overdue documents - Required actions  ==================================================== TRIP MANAGEMENT SCREEN ====================================================  Truck driver trip system:  Features: - Start Trip / End Trip - Route tracking (simple UI) - Trip history - Toll estimation - Fuel expense tracking - Loading / unloading notes  Trip Cards: - Date - Route - Earnings (optional) - Expenses - Stops  ==================================================== VEHICLE MANAGEMENT ====================================================  Truck profile system:  - Truck details (model, number) - Service history - Repair records - Tire status - Fuel usage logs - Maintenance schedule  Vehicle Health Score: - Engine - Tires - Brakes - Battery  ==================================================== FUEL & REST SUPPORT ====================================================  India highway support system:  Show nearby: - Fuel stations - Dhabas / rest stops - Mechanics - Truck parking zones  Features: - Distance-based listing - "Open 24/7" tags - Call button  ==================================================== HEALTH & EMERGENCY SYSTEM ====================================================  Truck driver safety module:  - Blood group - Medical conditions - Emergency contacts - SOS button (high priority) - Accident alert workflow  Emergency actions: - Call ambulance - Call police - Share live location - Notify fleet owner/family  ==================================================== POLICY & GOVERNMENT UPDATES ====================================================  Truck-specific regulations:  - Transport rule updates - Highway regulations - Toll changes - Insurance updates - Permit rules - State border regulations  Each update includes: - Simple summary - “Explain in local language” button - Impact level (High / Medium / Low)  ==================================================== AI ASSISTANT (TRUCK DRIVER FOCUS) ====================================================  AI assistant for real operational support:  - Explain documents - Explain fines or rules - Help with breakdown situations - Find nearest repair or fuel - Compliance questions - Trip planning help  Voice support is important.  ==================================================== LANGUAGE SUPPORT (INDIA FIRST) ====================================================  Must support: - Hindi - Telugu - Tamil - Kannada - Marathi - Bengali  Features: - Full UI translation - Voice input in regional languages - “Explain in my language” button everywhere - Simple icon-based navigation for low literacy users  ==================================================== DESIGN REQUIREMENTS ====================================================  - Mobile-first (primary) - Desktop dashboard (fleet owner view optional) - Large touch-friendly buttons - Offline mode indicators - Minimal typing required - Fast navigation (1–2 taps max) - Clear hierarchy of urgency  ==================================================== FINAL GOAL ====================================================  DriverOS India must feel like:  "A digital assistant and control system that helps Indian truck drivers manage legal compliance, documents, trips, vehicle health, and survival needs on highways — in one simple platform."  The system should immediately communicate:  - Organization of all paperwork - Prevention of fines and legal issues - Support during long highway trips - Emergency readiness - Reduction of dependency on brokers or paper systems
